ACME LOGISTICS GMBH — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 2.1 — approved by the Managing Director on 12 March 2025.
Reviewed annually.

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and systems operated by ACME
Logistics GmbH.

2. RISK MANAGEMENT
The IT Manager maintains a risk register. Risks are reviewed once a year and
scored on a five-point scale for likelihood and impact. Treatment decisions are
recorded and signed off by the Managing Director.

3. INCIDENT HANDLING
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately.
The IT Manager classifies each incident as Low, Medium or High and escalates
High incidents to the Managing Director. A log of incidents is kept.

4. BACKUPS
Backups of all production servers are taken nightly and retained for 30 days.
Backups are stored on a separate NAS device in the same building.

5. ACCESS CONTROL
Each employee receives an individual account. Administrator rights are granted
only to IT staff. Passwords must be at least ten characters long and are changed
every 90 days.

6. STAFF AWARENESS
New joiners receive a security briefing during onboarding.

7. SUPPLIERS
IT services may be procured from approved vendors.

8. PHYSICAL SECURITY
The office is locked outside business hours and the server room is accessible
only with a key held by IT staff.
